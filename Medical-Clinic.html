Everything you need to know before your visit to Nordic Medical Clinic.

No appointment is required — we welcome walk-in patients daily. However, booking an appointment in advance ensures you are seen at your preferred time with minimal waiting. You can book via our website, call us, or visit directly at 494 San Andres St, Malate, Manila.
We offer a comprehensive range of outpatient medical services including: General Consultation, Laboratory Testing (CBC, blood chemistry, urinalysis), Medical Certificates, Diagnostic Services (X-ray, ECG), Pre-Employment Physical Exams, Annual Executive Check-Ups, and Pediatric Consultations.
We are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. We are open on most public holidays — please call us at +63 2 8562 7831 to confirm availability on specific holidays.
Most routine laboratory results are available on the same day, typically within 3–5 hours. Specialized tests may take 1–3 business days. We will notify you when your results are ready and provide a full review with one of our physicians.
Yes, we accept PhilHealth for eligible services. We also work with select HMO providers. Please bring your card and ID when you visit, and our front desk staff will verify your coverage prior to your consultation.
For fasting blood tests (e.g., fasting blood sugar, lipid panel), please fast for 8–12 hours before your test. Water is allowed. For urine tests, collect a mid-stream sample in a clean container. If you have specific requirements for your test, our staff will advise you at the time of booking.

Built on Trust, Driven by Care

Nordic Medical Clinic was founded with a single mission: to bring fast, reliable, and genuinely caring healthcare to the people of Manila. Located at 494 San Andres Street in Malate, we've grown from a small general practice into a trusted multi-service clinic serving thousands of patients annually.

Inspired by the Scandinavian philosophy of clean spaces, efficiency, and patient-centered care, we designed every element of our clinic — from the physical environment to our clinical workflows — to make your healthcare experience as smooth and reassuring as possible.

Precision

Accurate diagnostics and evidence-based medicine at every visit.

General Consultation

For: All patients, all ages

Our general consultation service covers a wide range of health concerns — from acute illnesses like fever and infections to chronic disease management and preventive care. Our physicians are trained to listen, diagnose accurately, and prescribe appropriate treatments.

1

Registration

Check in at the front desk — walk-ins and appointments welcome.

2

Triage & Vitals

Nurse records your blood pressure, temperature, weight, and chief complaint.

3

Physician Consultation

A licensed physician conducts a thorough examination and assessment.

4

Prescription & Follow-Up

Receive your diagnosis, prescription, and care plan.

Laboratory Services

For: Patients requiring blood or urine testing

We offer a comprehensive panel of clinical laboratory examinations including complete blood count (CBC), fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, liver and kidney function tests, urinalysis, fecalysis, and more. Results are available on the same day for most standard tests.

1

Test Request Form

Bring your doctor's request or request tests at our clinic directly.

2

Sample Collection

Blood draw or specimen collection by our trained phlebotomist.

3

Processing

Samples analyzed in our in-house laboratory within 3–5 hours.

4

Results & Interpretation

Results released with physician review and explanation.

Drug Testing (DOLE-Compliant)

For: Employees, job applicants, OFWs, students

We conduct urine drug screening compliant with DOLE Department Order No. 53-03. Our drug test screens for commonly abused substances including methamphetamine (shabu), marijuana, and other controlled substances. Results are confidential and officially recognized by employers and government agencies.

DOLE-Compliant Confidential Same-Day Results
1

Registration

Present 1 valid ID and complete the drug test request form.

2

Specimen Collection

Urine sample collected under supervised chain-of-custody procedure.

3

Laboratory Analysis

Sample processed using validated ELISA or RIA method in our lab.

4

Certified Results Released

Official drug test certificate ready within 1–2 hours.
